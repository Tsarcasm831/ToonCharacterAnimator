# Disney Kingdom Clash Lore

This document outlines the narrative used for the prototype.

## Disclaimer
This is a fan-made project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Walt Disney Company.

## Story Premise
The Realms of Story have fractured. Each Disney world—Frozen, Moana, Toy Story and others—has been overrun by chaotic anomalies called **Disruptions**. These distortions are glitches in the weave of the tales. Heroes from across the realms are summoned to mend the narrative using their unique magic.

Players defend the realms by summoning heroes, clearing Disruptions, and restoring balance. As a realm stabilizes, its page in the **Codex of Tales** repairs itself, unlocking more heroes and new challenges.

## Progression
- **Fragments** – Clearing Disruptions awards fragments tied to the realm in play.
- **Page Shards** – Collect enough fragments to restore a page of the Codex and unlock new heroes.
- **Story Arc Stages** – Major battles themed to each realm that cap off a restoration, granting the ability to summon its guardian heroes.

## Terminology Changes
- **Enemies** ⇒ **Disruptions**
- **Kill** ⇒ **Stabilize** or **Correct**
- **Summon** ⇒ **Restore Anchor**
- **Merge** ⇒ **Clarify Memory**
- **Ultimate** ⇒ **Story Arc Burst**

The goal is to keep all gameplay mechanics intact while framing actions around restoring stories rather than destroying foes.
